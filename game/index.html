THE VELVET VAULT

A quiet heist in three rooms — steal the Starlit Muse and slip out the green door.
Move WASD or arrow keys  ·  Throw a coin left-click (costs 1 coin — guards walk over to investigate)  ·  Jammer right-click (costs 2 coins — freezes the nearest camera for 6 seconds)  ·  Restart room R  ·  Start / continue Enter
Guards and cameras sweep their vision cones. Stay out of the light: being seen raises your alarm. At 100% the vault locks down and the run is over. Grab the glowing loot, then reach the green exit. Walls and crates block sight — plan your route before you move.
